What ChessNotate is not

  • Not a way to play chess. There's no AI/computer opponent.
  • Not a full PGN database manager. No library, tagging, or search across multiple games.
  • Not a deep analysis tool. The eval bar is an at-a-glance check, not a best-move search.
  • Comments, clock times, and variations aren't preserved on export, only moves and basic annotations (!, !?, ?, ??, !!, ?!) are.
